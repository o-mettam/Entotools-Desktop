1. Overview

EntoTools is a free desktop application for growing degree day (GDD) calculations, weather lookups, specimen labels, and a collection database. It has no accounts and does not send your data to us. We, the developers, never receive your collection, your searches, or anything else you enter.

2. Data Stored on Your Computer

Everything you enter stays on your computer:

  • Your collection, saved searches and settings are kept in a database file in your user data folder (on macOS, ~/Library/Application Support/EntoTools; on Windows, AppData\Local\EntoTools).
  • CSV backups of your collection are saved automatically to a folder you choose (by default Documents/EntoTools Backups). You can change the folder or turn automatic backups off in Settings → Data & backups.
  • A cache of weather lookups and map tiles, and a small log file, are kept in your user cache folder so the app is faster and partly usable offline. The log does not record searched locations.

Deleting the app does not delete these files. Your backups are ordinary CSV files that you own and can open in any spreadsheet program.

3. Internet Services

When you look up weather or a location, the app sends the place names or coordinates and dates you searched for, directly from your computer, to these services. They also see your computer's IP address, as with any internet request:

  • NOAA NCEI — historical US weather data.
  • Open-Meteo — historical and forecast weather, elevation, humidity.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — finding places and coordinates.
  • Sunrise-Sunset.org — sunset times.
  • OpenStreetMap — map images, only when you open the collection map.
  • GitHub — only when the app checks for updates or you open the Service Status page.

We never send your name, contact details, collection or notes to any of them. Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy. Collection records, labels, CSV import/export and PDF printing work without an internet connection.

4. Cookies and Tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind. Inside its own window it uses one private cookie, created fresh at each launch, so that only the app's window can talk to the app's local data service. It never leaves your computer.

5. Feedback

If you report a problem or suggest a feature on the project's GitHub page, what you post there is public. Please don't include personal information or precise collecting localities you want to keep private.

6. Children's Privacy

EntoTools is intended for general, educational, and research use and is not directed at children under 13.
